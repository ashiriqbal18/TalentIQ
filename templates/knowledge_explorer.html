Logic & Rule Definitions

The formal ideas behind the executable eligibility rules.

FORMAL SPECIFICATION

First-Order Logic

∀

If a candidate and job exist, experience is sufficient, and every mandatory condition is met, eligibility follows.

Eligibility implication 
∀c,j:
 Candidate(c) ∧ Job(j) ∧
 MeetsExperience(c,j) ∧
 MeetsEveryMandatoryRequirement(c,j)
 → Eligible(c,j)
Quantifiers express a rule for all candidates and jobs.

Implementation Boundary

FOL and DL document the logical model. The live Eligibility Checker executes finite positive Horn clauses through Forward and Backward Chaining. No general-purpose FOL/DL theorem prover is running.

04 / REASONING METHODS

How Inference Works

Two proof strategies, one rule base, explainable results.

Forward Chaining

Data-driven reasoning
01 Start with known candidate and job facts
02 Fire applicable Horn rules in rounds
03 Derive facts until a fixed point is reached
If all mandatory facts are proved, derive eligibility.

Backward Chaining

Goal-driven reasoning
01 Start with eligible(candidate, job)
02 Expand the goal into supporting rule premises
03 Verify subgoals using known evidence
An unproved goal is not necessarily false.
Incomplete knowledge matters

Unrecorded skills or qualifications do not prove absence. The outcome can be Insufficient Information. Preferred conditions never block eligibility.
