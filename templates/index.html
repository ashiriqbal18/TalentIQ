PROJECT OVERVIEW

Making Job Eligibility Decisions Intelligent & Transparent

Traditional recruitment systems often rely on manual screening or basic keyword filtering. TalentIQ introduces knowledge-based reasoning to evaluate job eligibility through explicit rules.

Structured Knowledge

Organize candidates, job roles, qualifications, skills, certifications and experience using structured knowledge representations.

Logical Reasoning

Apply First-Order Logic, Description Logic, and Horn Clause rules to derive meaningful eligibility conclusions.

Explainable Results

Understand why a candidate meets or fails specific job requirements using clear reasoning traces and rule explanations.

CORE CAPABILITIES

Everything Needed for Intelligent Eligibility Evaluation

A unified system for managing recruitment knowledge and performing automated rule-based evaluation.

Candidate Management

Dynamically add, edit, view, and delete candidate profiles with their qualifications, skills, experience, and certifications.

Job Role Management

Create unlimited job role definitions with flexible mandatory and preferred eligibility requirements.

Eligibility Checking

Compare candidate attributes with job requirements to determine eligibility through logical rules.

Forward Chaining

Start with available facts and repeatedly apply inference rules to derive new eligibility conclusions.

Backward Chaining

Start with an eligibility hypothesis and verify whether its supporting facts and conditions are satisfied.

Knowledge Representation

Explore Frames, Semantic Networks, First-Order Logic, Description Logic, and Horn Clause representations.
